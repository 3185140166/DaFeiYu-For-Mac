## Hand readability refinement v2

The current runtime sprites use AI-edited derivatives in `hand-refinement/`:
`idle-hands-v2.png` and `happy-hands-v2.png`. Both expose warm peach palms with
readable thumbs/fingers and orange sleeve trim. Generated using built-in image_gen;
full prompts and integration notes are in `hand-refinement/PROMPTS.md`.
The high-resolution edits are uniformly scaled to 640×640 for the app. Surrounding
linework may differ slightly due to image generation. Original WebP sources above
are retained unchanged. Rebuild originals with `swift scripts/prepare_hoodie_art.swift --original`.
These edited sprites and derived icons retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the same credits.

Three-quarter walk adaptation (2026-10-28): the relaxed front idle and two eight-frame
side-facing sheets in artwork-src/claude-hoodie/motion-v3/ were edited with built-in
image_gen from the earlier licensed hoodie derivative. The sheets are sliced,
normalized and face-aligned to 896×640. Near-transparent saturated fringe is removed
and an outer transparent safety gutter retained. The runtime uses these illustrated
frames and a short crossfade to the front pose; the previous frontal leg rig is
superseded. docs/hoodie-walk-preview.gif is exported from the actual frames.
Sources, frames, derived icons and previews retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the credits above.
Full generation prompts: artwork-src/claude-hoodie/motion-v3/PROMPTS.md.


Alternating gait v4 (2026-10-28): motion-v4/walk-right-alternating.png was edited
with built-in image_gen from the earlier sheet. Final sprites preserve eight complete illustrated poses per direction, with
connected-component fragment removal only. The earlier extracted-leg processing
was removed because it damaged the skirt and thighs. Prompts and processing
notes: artwork-src/claude-hoodie/motion-v4/PROMPTS.md. These derivatives retain
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the attribution above. Runtime default speed is 48 points/s;
a full two-step cycle lasts about 1.2 seconds.

Sources and processing notes: artwork-src/claude-hoodie/motion-v5/PROMPTS.md.
All derivatives retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the attribution above.
